Chapter 11, Aggregate expenditures

The spending multiplier, round by round

A business spends more on new equipment. That spending becomes someone's income. They spend part of it (the MPC) and save the rest, and their spending becomes someone else's income. Click Next round to follow the money, or Show all rounds to jump to the end.

Chapter 15, Money creation

How banks create money

Someone deposits new cash in Bank A. The bank keeps the required share as reserves and lends out the rest. The borrower spends the loan, and the seller deposits it in Bank B, which does the same thing. Each new loan is new money. Click Next bank to follow the chain.
